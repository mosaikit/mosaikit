### Added

- Required and optional dependencies between extensions (MK-132): the manifest declares, next to
  `requires`, the extensions it uses `optional`ly, each with a range of versions, and they never
  prevent installation or start. The marketplace refuses an installation whose required extension
  is missing or out of its range, and an update that takes an extension out of the range of an
  installed extension, naming them; `POST /api/v1/marketplace/operations` installs several packages
  together. `DELETE /api/v1/extensions/<id>` removes an extension, refused, like turning it off in
  an organization, while an active extension requires it.
