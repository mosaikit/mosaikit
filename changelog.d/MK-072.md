### Added

- Console of the platform administrators (MK-072): **Console** shows organizations active and
  suspended, people, active extensions, the metrics of the kernel and the security events of the
  last 24 hours, such as refused sign-ins, now recorded in the audit log of the platform
  (`account.sign-in.failed`); `GET /api/v1/platform/console`. Extensions add panels through the
  typed point `console.panel` of the shell, checked at installation.
- Organizations are suspended and reactivated (`PUT` and `DELETE
  /api/v1/organizations/{slug}/suspension`): their members sign in to them by no means and their
  sessions in them end, while data and access keys stay. **Organizations** creates one with a guided
  flow (name, sign-in method, first administrator, review).
- **People** lists every person of the installation with their organizations
  (`GET /api/v1/platform/accounts`), and replaces the page **Accounts**. Organizations and People
  follow one model: metrics on top, filters above the table, actions on each row and the detail of a
  row in a side pane.
- Every administration page follows that model: Organization apps, Permissions (profiles, people and
  teams as tables, edited in the side pane), Credentials and Installation credentials, Quotas,
  Extensions, Service identities, External origins and Platform.
