### Added

- An extension knows whether another one is present and active in an organization (MK-133): the
  `ExtensionPresence` bean of `kernel-api` answers in the organization of the request, with the
  version, and `context.extensions` of `@mosaikit/sdk` answers the same in the frontend without a
  request, kept current through the real-time topic `extensions`. Installing, updating, removing and
  turning an extension on or off raise the platform events `extension.activated` and
  `extension.deactivated` in every organization concerned, also for changes made while the kernel
  was stopped. `GET /api/v1/shell/extensions/active` lists the active extensions for the shell.
