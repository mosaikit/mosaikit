### Added

- Offboarding a member with the transfer of their resources (MK-071): before removing a member, the
  administrators see the resources they own, per extension (`GET .../members/{email}/resources`), and
  choose another member who takes them over (`DELETE .../members/{email}?transferTo=…`, 409 without it
  when the member owns resources); the resources change owner in the registry with their grants
  intact, and extensions follow through the extension point `OwnershipTransfers` of `kernel-api`.
  A platform administrator removes a member from **Permissions**, **People**, choosing the new owner.
  The transfer is audited.
