### Added

- Branding per organization, with a live editor of the theme tokens (MK-074): the administrators of
  an organization set on the new **Branding** page a display name, an accent color, the colors of
  the light and dark schemes, shown on the open application before they are saved, and a logo for
  light and for dark backgrounds, an icon and a favicon (PNG, JPEG, WebP, ICO or SVG, up to 2 MB,
  kept with the files of the installation). Members see them after sign-in and on the sign-in page
  of the organization (`/?organization=<slug>`); a theme chosen by the person wins over them, and
  they win over the theme of the installation. `GET /api/v1/organizations/{slug}/branding` (public),
  `PUT` of it and `PUT`/`DELETE`/`GET /api/v1/organizations/{slug}/branding/images/{kind}`.
  `@mosaikit/ui`: `registerTheme` takes the theme to fill from, and `themeDefinition` gives the
  colors of a theme.
