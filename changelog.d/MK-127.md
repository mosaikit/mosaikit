### Added

- Credentials of the installation (MK-127): platform administrators keep, in **Installation
  credentials** or with `/api/v1/platform/credentials`, the keys and passwords of services shared by
  every organization, encrypted like those of organizations and bound to the scope of the
  installation (migration V31; the member role of requests has no right on the table). Extension
  backends read them with `Secrets.resolve`, an addition to `kernel-api`: the credential of the
  organization of the request wins when it has one with the same name, and outside any organization
  the one of the installation applies. Changes and reads are audited in the log of the platform; the
  rotation of the master key covers them.
