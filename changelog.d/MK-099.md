### Added

- Apps keep their view in the address of the page (MK-099): `context.address` of `@mosaikit/sdk`
  reads and writes a state after the route of the app (a sub-path such as
  `/app/files/folders/42`, a query, a fragment) and tells the app when the person moves with the
  back and forward buttons, also in isolated frontends. Any path under the route of an app opens
  that app, and the address opened before signing in, also through the identity provider of an
  organization, is the one opened after.
