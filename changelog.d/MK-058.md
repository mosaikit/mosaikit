### Added

- Requests for access (MK-058): a person refused a resource sends a request with a message from the
  page that refused them, `<mk-empty-state kind="denied" resource="…">` of `@mosaikit/ui` offering
  **Request access**, or `requestAccess` of `@mosaikit/sdk` (topic `shell.request-access`). The owner,
  or the administrators for a resource of the organization, are notified in-app and approve, with the
  level asked or another, or decline from the page **Requests for access** of the shell; an approval
  grants the level with the sharing model, and the requester is notified either way. The answer to a
  request is the same whether the resource exists or not. Every request and decision is audited. New
  endpoints `/api/v1/access-requests`, migration V47.
