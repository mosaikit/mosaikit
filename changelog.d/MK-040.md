### Added

- Global search with pills (MK-040): the search box of the top bar opens the results of the whole
  organization on the page `/search`, with **All** and one pill per provider, a page at a time. The
  kernel provides people and teams, found with PostgreSQL full-text search among those the person
  would see elsewhere; extensions add pills with the point `search.provider` (a path of their backend,
  called with the credentials of the person), and the kernel drops their results that name a resource
  the person may not read. A provider slower than `mosaikit.search.timeout` (3 s) is shown as
  incomplete and the others are shown anyway. New endpoints `GET /api/v1/search` and
  `GET /api/v1/search/providers`; the combobox of the top bar is now named **Search**.
