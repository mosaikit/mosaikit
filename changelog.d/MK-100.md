### Added

- Views of extensions embedded in the pages of other sites (MK-100): the embed mode of the shell,
  `/embed/<slug>/...`, shows a view without the app bar and the top bar: a resource that the
  organization shares with the public (`/public/<extension>/<type>/<id>`, without an account), a
  resource opened with a link of the organization (`/shared/<secret>`), or the view of an app with its
  state in the address (`/app/...`, for a person who is signed in). The administrators of an
  organization list the sites that may frame its views in **Embedded views** or with
  `/api/v1/organizations/{slug}/embed-sites` (audited, migration V49); the kernel sends them in
  `frame-ancestors` for those addresses only, and every other answer stays refused to frames
  (`X-Frame-Options: DENY`, `frame-ancestors 'none'`). A request without an account that names an
  organization in `X-Mosaikit-Public` reads, through the routes of an extension open to links, the
  resources that the organization shares with the public (`GET /api/v1/sharing/public/...`), also
  through a folder above them, never one in the trash.
