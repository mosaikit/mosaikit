### Added

- Free tags on the resources of extensions (MK-097): the `Tags` bean of `kernel-api` attaches,
  replaces and reads the tags of a registered resource, stored in lowercase without duplicates, and
  finds the resources of a type that carry any of some tags. `GET|PUT /api/v1/resources/tags/{extension}/{type}/{id}`,
  `GET /api/v1/resources/tags/{extension}/{type}?tag=` and `GET /api/v1/resources/tags/suggestions`
  give the frontends the same tags and suggestions (the tags used in the organization, most used
  first), only on the resources the person may read; `@mosaikit/sdk` adds `resourceTags`,
  `replaceTags`, `suggestTags`, `taggedResources` and `normalizeTag`.
