### Added

- A trash for the resources of extensions (MK-095): an extension moves a resource to the trash with
  the new `Trash` bean instead of deleting it, and the resources inside it go with it. While in the
  trash it gives no level to anyone and its links answer not found. Its owner restores it from the
  page **Trash** of the shell (`GET /api/v1/trash`, `POST /api/v1/trash/{id}/restore`), with its
  shares and links; the administrators of the organization see and restore those of everyone. After
  `mosaikit.trash.retention` (30 days, one day at least, also checked by the database), or with
  **Delete for good** (`DELETE /api/v1/trash/{id}`), the kernel removes it from the registry. The
  extensions are told with the events `resource.trashed`, `resource.restored` and `resource.purged`;
  the audit log records them.
