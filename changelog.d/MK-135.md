### Added

- References between resources of different extensions degrade gracefully when one is absent
  (MK-135): the `ResourceReferences` bean of `kernel-api` and `POST /api/v1/resources/references/resolve`
  resolve a reference kept by one extension to a resource registered by another, or to why it is not
  available (`extension-absent`, `deleted`, `forbidden`), and tell administrators which extension to
  install or turn on. `@mosaikit/sdk` adds `resolveReferences`, `watchReferences` (which resolves
  again when the other extension comes or goes, without a reload) and the element
  `<mk-reference-placeholder>`, in English and Italian, which the shell defines for extensions loaded
  as modules. Nothing is deleted or changed while an extension is absent.
