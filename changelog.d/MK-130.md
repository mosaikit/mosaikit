### Added

- Deleting an organization (MK-130): a platform administrator deletes one in the console page
  **Organizations**, or with `DELETE /api/v1/organizations/{slug}?confirmation=<slug>`, after writing
  its slug again. It appears nowhere at once, its people lose access (their sessions in it end, their
  access keys in it are revoked), its email domains are released, and the extensions subscribed to
  `organization.deleted` are told, to release what they keep outside the installation. Its data, and
  those of its extensions, stay for `mosaikit.organizations.removal-retention` (30 days, one day at
  least); then a job of the kernel removes them and queues the content of its files for the clean-up
  of the store. The audit log records `organization.deleted` and `organization.removed`. The
  published views leave out deleted organizations.
