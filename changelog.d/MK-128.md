### Added

- External origins in the content security policy (MK-128): platform administrators list, in
  **External origins** or with `/api/v1/platform/content-security-policy`, the services outside the
  installation that the shell and the frontends of extensions may contact from the browser, and
  `mosaikit.content-security-policy.allowed-origins` lists more in the configuration. Each origin is
  `scheme://host[:port]` (one leading `*.` at most) and goes to `connect-src` and `img-src` only;
  everything else stays blocked. Changes are audited in the log of the platform; the table (migration
  V32) is out of reach of requests, so neither administrators of organizations nor extensions can
  widen the policy. The kernel now builds the policy itself (`mosaikit.content-security-policy.enabled`,
  on in production), the same header as before when no origin is listed.
