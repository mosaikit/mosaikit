### Added

- Every distribution accepts settings of the kernel and of extensions without changing its files
  (MK-129): the Helm chart renders `kernel.extraSettings` and `extensions.<id>.settings` as
  environment variables of the kernel, secret values by reference to a Kubernetes secret
  (`valueFrom.secretKeyRef`), with `kernel.extraEnv` and `kernel.extraEnvFrom` for more; Docker
  Compose reads `mosaikit.env` (see `mosaikit.env.example`) and documents a file of secret settings
  mounted as a secret of Compose; the installation and portable distributions document the setting
  of an extension in `config/application.properties`.
