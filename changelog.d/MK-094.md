### Added

- Favorites across extensions (MK-094): a person marks a resource of any extension, registered with
  the kernel, as favorite and finds it in **Favorites** of the account menu, which opens it in its
  extension. A favorite the person may no longer read leaves the list; one whose extension is absent
  stays as the placeholder of MK-135 and opens again when it comes back. `GET /api/v1/favorites` and
  `GET`, `PUT`, `DELETE /api/v1/favorites/{extension}/{type}/{id}`; `@mosaikit/sdk` adds
  `markFavorite`, `unmarkFavorite`, `isFavorite` and `listFavorites`.
