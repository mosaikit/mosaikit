### Added

- A home page of widgets contributed by extensions (MK-101): extensions contribute to `home.widget`,
  an extension point of the kernel typed by a JSON Schema (MK-134), with a title, a custom element,
  the roles it appears for and whether anonymous visitors see it (`public`), a size and an order.
  The marketplace refuses a widget that does not match, with the reason; the kernel gives each person
  only the widgets of their roles, and visitors, under the sign-in page, only the public ones
  (`GET /api/v1/shell/home/public`). A widget of an extension that stops being active in the
  organization disappears without a reload (MK-133).
