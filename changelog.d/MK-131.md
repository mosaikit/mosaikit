### Added

- Java extensions bring their own libraries (MK-131): `mvn package` on the parent POM of extensions
  copies the runtime dependencies of an extension into `lib/`, next to its JAR, and lists them in
  `manifest.yaml` under `libraries` with their Maven coordinates and SHA-256 digests; the libraries
  of the kernel, managed by the parent POM in its versions with scope `provided`, stay out of the
  package. The launcher adds the libraries to the kernel with the JAR, once each, and rebuilds the
  kernel when they change. The launcher, the kernel and the marketplace refuse, naming the library
  and both versions, a library that the kernel or another extension has in another version, and a
  library that is a Quarkus extension. The libraries of the kernel are listed in
  `docs/development/kernel-libraries.md`.
