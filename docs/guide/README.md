# Mosaikit — Guides

Guides for the people who use Mosaikit, one per role. They follow the English interface; the Italian one
has the same pages and places.

| Guide | For |
|---|---|
| [user.md](user.md) | Everybody who works in a tenant: signing in, the shell, apps and items, sharing and public links, access requests, notifications, trash, account, search, shortcuts, accessibility |
| [tenant-manager.md](tenant-manager.md) | The Tenant Manager: people, groups, permission profiles, pending, plugins and their permissions, shares, usage and alerts, log, tenant settings and theme |
| [administrator.md](administrator.md) | The Administrator: first start and second factor, tenants and their lifecycle, sign-in of a tenant (local or OIDC), people of the installation, plugins, system settings, backups, maintenance, monitoring, log |

**Where people read them.** Help › **Guide of Mosaikit** opens the address set in System › Identity and
appearance › Identity and texts › **Guide**; when it is empty it shows the short guide built into the shell.
To give people these guides, publish them (an internal site, a wiki) and put their address there; a tenant
may set its own.

What changed in each version is in [CHANGELOG.md](../../CHANGELOG.md), shown in Help › **What is new**.

Developers and operators: [architecture.md](../architecture.md), [manual-tests.md](../manual-tests.md).
