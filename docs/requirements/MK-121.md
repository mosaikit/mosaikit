---
id: MK-121
title: Companion services started by the portable launcher
area: distribution
priority: P2
status: planned
acceptance:
  - A distribution declares companion services with their command, ports, health check and order.
  - The launcher picks free ports, starts the services in order, waits for each health check, and
    passes the ports to the configuration of the kernel.
  - Stopping the launcher stops the services in reverse order, and a failed health check stops the
    start with a clear message.
---

A product built on the portable distribution may need further local services next to the kernel and
its database. It extends the launcher of MK-016, which starts the kernel and PostgreSQL, so that it
starts these services in order, on free ports, and stops them with the kernel.
