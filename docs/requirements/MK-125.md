---
id: MK-125
title: Sign-out and administrators end sessions on the server
area: security
priority: P1
status: done
acceptance:
  - After sign-out, the same session cookie replayed is refused at once.
  - Suspending a person or removing them from their organization ends their sessions within seconds.
  - A person sees their active sessions in their settings and ends any of them.
---

The session of local accounts is an encrypted cookie that the kernel does not record: after sign-out
the browser forgets it, but a copy of it still opens the platform until it expires, up to eight hours
later. Services that check the session on every request (a gateway in front of data) inherit the
same gap. The kernel keeps a server-side record of sessions, so that sign-out, suspension and
removal end them at once, and people can see and end their own.

## History

- 2026-10-13: written after the proofs of a product built on Mosaikit, where a cookie replayed after
  sign-out still opened the data behind the gateway of the product.
- 2026-10-13: in progress — server-side record of sessions, checked on every request.
- 2026-10-13: done — the kernel keeps sessions in `account_session` (migration V19): the cookie of the
  shell is a random value kept as a digest, realm sessions are recorded from the `sid` of their
  tokens; signing out, ending a session in the settings, a suspension (new, by platform
  administrators) and the removal from an organization refuse them at once, with "remember me"
  tokens revoked. Tests `ServerSessionTest`, `SessionRegistryTest`, `FederatedSessionTest` and
  e2e `21-sessions`.
- 2026-10-14: fix (#97) — behind a reverse proxy that terminates TLS the session cookie lacked
  `Secure`: the cookies follow the scheme Quarkus resolved, the one forwarded by a trusted proxy
  included, Docker Compose and the Helm chart believe `X-Forwarded-Proto` from the proxies they
  name, and `mosaikit.accounts.secure-cookies` makes them always `Secure`. Tests
  `SecureCookieBehindProxyTest`, `SecureCookieUntrustedProxyTest`, `SecureCookiesTest`.
- 2026-10-14: the real-time connection of a page closes when its session ends (issue #98): the
  kernel closes it with code `4401` at once, and the other kernels within 5 seconds; tests
  `LiveSessionEndTest` and `FederatedSessionTest`.
