depends: [MK-032, MK-038]
acceptance:
  - A person creates an event for a team and its members receive the invitation in the activity
    feed.
  - Reminders arrive as notifications before the event.
---

The extension app-calendar offers personal and team calendars, with events, invitations and
reminders in the activity feed; meetings with audio and video are not part of it.
