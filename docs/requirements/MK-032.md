id: MK-032
title: Teams as groups of the organization, with roles and guests
area: security
priority: P1
status: done
depends: [MK-017, MK-019]
acceptance:
  - An owner creates a team, adds members and guests and changes their roles; every change is in the
    audit.
  - A guest sees only the teams where they were added, and none of the other data of the
    organization.
  - A row of an extension shared with a team is readable by its members and by no one else.
---

Teams are groups of the organization in the kernel, public or private, with members who are owners,
members or guests; a guest is a person of another organization admitted to that team only.
Extensions read groups through kernel-api, and row-level security has a policy for data shared with
a group. A guest needs an account of the installation and signs in with a password. See ADR-0027 and
ADR-0033.

## History

- 2026-10-13: the documents shared with a team moved onto the one sharing model (MK-055,
  ADR-0037): each is a registered resource with a grant to the team at the level edit (migration
  V28); they stay readable by the members of the team only, not by the administrators of the
  organization for being administrators, and the sharing dialog can share them with more people.
- 2026-10-14: `People` in kernel-api (#105): the people and teams of the organization for the Java
  code of extensions, read-only and for one organization, private teams and groups included, chats
  never, a guest limited to their teams; tests in `PeopleTest`.
- 2026-10-14: a person added to a team, changed or removed raises an event of the platform (MK-085,
  #103).
- 2026-10-14: guests reach the API of an extension once a team of theirs or an exception grants them
  one of its permissions (MK-056) or one of its resources is theirs or shared with them or their
  teams (MK-055), and there only what its checks give them; before, every extension API refused
  them (issue #92). Test `GuestExtensionApiTest`.
