---
id: MK-133
title: An extension knows whether another one is present and active, and is told when it changes
area: extensions
priority: P1
status: done
depends: [MK-085, MK-030, MK-132]
decisions: [ADR-0041]
acceptance:
  - On the server, a bean of `kernel-api` tells an extension whether another extension is present
    and active in the organization of the request (installed, compatible with the kernel and with
    its dependencies, and its app turned on in that organization), and gives its version.
  - In the frontend, `@mosaikit/sdk` gives the same answer for the organization of the session,
    without a request to the server for each question.
  - When another extension becomes active or inactive in an organization (installed, removed,
    updated, turned on or off), the extension backends subscribed to it are told through the
    platform events of MK-085, and open frontends are told without a reload.
  - An extension can ask about any extension, whether or not it declares it as a dependency; the
    answer says nothing about an extension that is not installed beyond its absence.
---

Extensions are independent of each other (ADR-0041): one that can use another works without it. To
do so it needs to know, when it decides what to show or which path to take, whether the other one is
there; and to react when an administrator installs it or turns it off, so that a page does not keep
offering what has just disappeared.
