---
id: MK-068
title: "Invitations to join an organization, and a personal workspace for self-registered people"
area: kernel
priority: P2
status: proposed
acceptance:
  - An invitation link works once and is refused after use or after seven days.
  - Accepting an invitation with an existing account adds the organization to its memberships, with
    the role chosen in the invitation.
  - Self-registration creates a personal organization with the person as its only member, and it
    stays when the person joins another organization.
---

Organizations are joined by invitation, so that nobody takes the name of an organization before it
does, and accepting adds a membership instead of replacing one. People who register by themselves
get a personal workspace to use before anyone invites them. It extends MK-017, membership of several
organizations, with the way a person enters one.
