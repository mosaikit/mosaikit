---
id: MK-033
title: Presence of people
area: kernel
priority: P2
status: planned
depends: [MK-031]
acceptance:
  - The presence of a person changes for the others within a few seconds when they open or close
    their last page or choose another status.
  - People of other organizations see the presence only of the guests of their shared teams.
---

The kernel keeps the presence of each person (available, busy, away, offline) from the open pages
and from the choice of the person, and offers it to the extensions. See ADR-0028.
