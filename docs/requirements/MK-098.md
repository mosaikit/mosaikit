title: Who else has the same resource open
area: shell
priority: P1
status: proposed
depends: [MK-054]
acceptance:
  - Two people who open the same resource see each other's avatar, and it disappears when one of
    them closes it.
  - Two tabs of the same person count as one presence.
  - A person without read access to the resource cannot join its presence room.
---

Extensions show the avatars of the people who have the same resource open, so that people know who
else is working on it. It runs on the real-time channel and is limited to those allowed to read the
resource. It extends the presence of people of MK-033 to presence on a resource.
