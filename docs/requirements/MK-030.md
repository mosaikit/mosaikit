id: MK-030
title: Apps of the app bar per organization and role
area: extensions
priority: P1
status: done
depends: [MK-002, MK-025]
acceptance:
  - An app disabled for an organization does not appear in its app bar, and its API refuses the
    people of that organization.
  - The order and the pinned apps set by the administrator are the same for every person of the
    organization.
---

Administrators of an organization choose which apps of the active extensions appear in its app bar,
in which order and for which roles (teams come with MK-032); pinned apps cannot be hidden by people.
When all the apps of an extension are off, its API and its documents refuse the organization.

## History

- 2026-10-13: the apps per role moved onto permissions (MK-056, ADR-0038): instead of roles, an app
  is shown to everyone or restricted to the people who hold its permission `use-<app>`, which the
  administrators hold and profiles, teams and personal exceptions grant. Settings saved for the
  administrators only became restricted, with the same effect; the others show to members and
  administrators, and guests see an app only when its permission is granted to them.
