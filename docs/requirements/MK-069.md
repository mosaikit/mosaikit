---
id: MK-069
title: Policies of an organization for members coming from other organizations
area: kernel
priority: P1
status: proposed
acceptance:
  - The hosting organization sets the highest role a member from another organization can hold, and
    a higher role cannot be assigned.
  - The hosting organization can require a second factor, and a member without one is refused there
    but not in their home organization.
  - Removing such a member leaves their account and their home membership intact.
---

A person with one account belongs to a home organization and joins others from outside. The hosting
organization decides the highest role such a member can hold and how strongly they authenticate,
without affecting their home organization. It extends the guests of MK-032, admitted to one team, to
members of a whole organization.
