---
id: MK-135
title: References between resources of different extensions degrade gracefully when one is absent
area: extensions
priority: P2
status: planned
depends: [MK-054, MK-133]
decisions: [ADR-0037, ADR-0041]
acceptance:
  - A resource of one extension refers to a resource of another through the registry of resources,
    for example a dashboard widget that shows a map, and the kernel resolves the reference to the
    resource or to the reason it is not available (extension absent, resource deleted, no right to
    read it).
  - When the extension of the referred resource is missing or turned off in the organization, the
    referring resource shows a placeholder that says what is missing and, to administrators, which
    extension to install or turn on; the rest of the resource works.
  - When that extension comes back, the reference works again, also in an open page without a
    reload, with nothing to repair by hand.
  - Neither the referring nor the referred resource is deleted or changed because an extension is
    absent; references are kept as they are.
  - The frontend SDK, `@mosaikit/sdk`, gives extensions the placeholder as a component, so that
    every extension shows an absence the same way.
---

Products composed from common extensions (ADR-0041) mix resources of different extensions: a
dashboard shows a map, a process reads a dataset of the catalog. When the extension that owns the
referred resource is not installed, or is turned off in an organization, the referring resource must
neither break nor lose the reference: an administrator may turn the other extension on again
tomorrow.
