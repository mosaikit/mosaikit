---
id: MK-034
title: Channels with posts, threads, mentions and reactions (app-teams)
area: extensions
priority: P1
status: done
depends: [MK-031, MK-032, MK-038]
acceptance:
  - A post in a channel appears at once for the members who have the channel open, and a reply goes
    to its thread.
  - A mention notifies the person, or the members of the team, in the activity feed.
  - A private channel is visible only to its members.
---

The extension app-teams gives each team a General channel and other standard or private channels,
with posts, threads, mentions of people and teams, reactions and cards published by extensions. A
private channel is a group of the team in the kernel; the cards of extensions come with the chats
(MK-036). See ADR-0027 and ADR-0033.
