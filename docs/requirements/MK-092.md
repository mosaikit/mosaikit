title: "Notices, news, maintenance banner and maintenance mode"
area: shell
priority: P2
status: proposed
acceptance:
  - An administrator publishes a dated notice with a detail page, listed in a public list and in a
    feed.
  - A maintenance banner shows at the top of public and signed-in pages between its start and end
    time only.
  - With maintenance mode on, every open shell shows a full-screen notice with its message, and API
    calls of people other than administrators get a problem detail saying so.
  - Turning maintenance mode off removes the notice without reloading the page.
---

Organizations announce news and scheduled maintenance to their members and visitors without
switching the product off. During upgrades or incidents operators stop people from working, while
administrators can still sign in, and everyone is told why.
