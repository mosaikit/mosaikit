---
id: MK-117
title: Export of a whole organization and an exit procedure
area: kernel
priority: P2
status: proposed
acceptance:
  - An administrator exports an organization into one archive in documented open formats.
  - Extensions contribute their content to the archive through a declared export point.
  - Importing the archive into another installation recreates the organization, and the deletion of
    the original is recorded with a certificate.
---

Customers buy on condition that they can leave. The platform exports an organization (people,
memberships, files, documents and the content of extensions) in open formats, imports it elsewhere
and certifies the deletion of what remains.
