---
id: MK-081
title: "Governance of the installed extensions of an organization, uninstall included"
area: extensions
priority: P2
status: proposed
acceptance:
  - Turning an extension off for an organization hides it and refuses its calls, and turning it on
    restores it without reinstalling.
  - The page of an installed extension shows its version, origin, granted scopes, last use, response
    times and its entries in the audit.
  - Errors raised by an extension carry its name in the UI and in the logs.
  - Before uninstalling, the page lists the data the extension declares as its own and offers to
    export them; content that people created with it stays with the organization.
---

Administrators must decide who uses an extension, revoke a scope, stop it in an emergency and tell
whether a fault belongs to the extension or to the platform. Uninstalling must say beforehand what
data will be deleted and what remains. It extends MK-030, where the apps of an extension are turned
off per organization and role, to the whole extension and its lifecycle.
