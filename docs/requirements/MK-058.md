---
id: MK-058
title: Requests for access decided by owners or administrators
area: security
priority: P1
status: done
depends: [MK-055]
acceptance:
  - A person refused access sends a request with a message from the page that refused them.
  - The owner or the administrator receives a notification and approves or rejects the request from
    one list.
  - An approved request grants the access asked, and the requester is notified of the outcome either
    way.
---

People ask to join an organization or to open a resource they were refused. Someone entitled to
decide must see those requests in one place and answer them, and the person who asked must learn the
outcome.
