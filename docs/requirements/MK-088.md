---
id: MK-088
title: Notifications by mail and SMS with preferences per person and a daily digest
area: kernel
priority: P1
status: planned
depends: [MK-087]
acceptance:
  - A notification of a type the person chose to receive by mail arrives in the activity feed and in
    their mailbox, with a link back to the item.
  - A notification of a type the person chose to receive by SMS is handed to the configured SMS
    provider.
  - A person who chose a daily digest receives one mail a day listing the notifications of the day,
    and no single mail for them.
---

Some notifications, such as a failed job or a pending approval, must reach people who are not
looking at the application. Each person chooses, per notification type, whether it also comes by
mail or SMS, and people who receive many prefer one summary a day. It extends the activity feed of
MK-038 with these channels and the digest.
