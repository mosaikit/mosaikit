---
id: MK-076
title: "Usage and storage quotas per organization, metered and enforced"
area: kernel
priority: P1
status: done
acceptance:
  - An extension declares a metered measure in its manifest, and an administrator sets a limit for
    it per organization.
  - An extension asks the kernel whether an estimated consumption fits the remaining quota, and an
    action that would exceed it is refused with a problem detail naming the limit.
  - Storage counts the file service and the space that extensions report for their resources, and
    new data beyond the storage quota are refused.
  - The organization administrator sees consumption by measure, period and person, on screen and
    through the API, updated at least every hour.
---

Extensions consume metered resources such as storage, calls to external services and AI credits.
Limits per organization must be declared in one place, checked before an action and enforced with a
clear reason, instead of each extension implementing its own. Organizations must also see what they
consume, and installations offered as a service need the same figures to bill and to plan capacity.

## History

- 2026-10-13: written from the needs of a product built on Mosaikit, reviewed from its backlog; 5 of
  its requirements depend on it.
- 2026-10-13: in progress — usage and storage metered per organization, with quotas enforced.
- 2026-10-13: the kernel measures storage per organization, the files of the kernel and the space
  that extensions report for their resources (table `quota_space`), kept current by triggers
  (migration V27); limits per organization over the defaults of the installation
  (`mosaikit.quotas.*`), set by platform administrators; an upload beyond the storage limit is
  refused with a problem detail naming it, under a lock so that two uploads cannot both pass it
  (acceptance 3 for the files of the kernel); consumption by measure, period and person through
  `/api/v1/organizations/{slug}/quotas` and the Quotas page of the administrators (acceptance 4).
  Measures of extensions and the Java API wait for the review of the SDK.
- 2026-10-13: done — extensions declare measures in `measures` of their manifests (counter per
  month or gauge), shown and limited per organization with storage; the `Quotas` bean checks an
  amount, consumes a counter, sets a gauge and reports the space of a registered resource, which
  counts to storage with the files; a refusal is `403 Quota exceeded` naming the limit. Probe tests
  cover acceptance 1 to 3 for extensions.
