---
id: MK-076
title: "Usage and storage quotas per organization, metered and enforced"
area: kernel
priority: P1
status: planned
acceptance:
  - An extension declares a metered measure in its manifest, and an administrator sets a limit for
    it per organization.
  - An extension asks the kernel whether an estimated consumption fits the remaining quota, and an
    action that would exceed it is refused with a problem detail naming the limit.
  - Storage counts the file service and the space that extensions report for their resources, and
    new data beyond the storage quota are refused.
  - The organization administrator sees consumption by measure, period and person, on screen and
    through the API, updated at least every hour.
---

Extensions consume metered resources such as storage, calls to external services and AI credits.
Limits per organization must be declared in one place, checked before an action and enforced with a
clear reason, instead of each extension implementing its own. Organizations must also see what they
consume, and installations offered as a service need the same figures to bill and to plan capacity.
