---
id: MK-124
title: Service identities for internal callers of the APIs of extensions
area: security
priority: P1
status: in-progress
depends: [MK-060]
acceptance:
  - An administrator creates a service identity with a key and the APIs of the extensions it may
    call; a request with that key reaches those APIs without a person or an organization.
  - A service identity cannot sign in to the shell and is refused on every API it was not given.
  - Every call of a service identity is in the audit log, and revoking its key refuses the next call.
---

Some callers of the APIs of extensions are not people: a reverse proxy that asks whether a request
may pass, a server that checks a personal key or reads roles. Today every call to `/api/v1/ext/*` is
tied to a person in an organization, so such callers need a fake person in an organization of their
own. A service identity is a first-class caller: it has a key, a list of the APIs it may call, no
organization and no shell, and it is audited and revocable like a personal key.

## History

- 2026-10-13: written after the proofs of a product built on Mosaikit, which needed a person in an
  organization of its own for a gateway and a map server to call its extension.
- 2026-10-13: in progress — service identities with keys, limited to the APIs they are given.
