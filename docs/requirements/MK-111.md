---
id: MK-111
title: "Governance of AI use per organization: switch, budget, rate limit, cost and transcript"
area: ai
priority: P1
status: proposed
acceptance:
  - An organization administrator turns AI use off, and afterwards no assistant turn and no model
    call of an extension is made for that organization.
  - Each model call of the assistant or of an extension is metered and charged to the AI budget of
    the organization, and a call that would exceed the budget or the rate limit is refused before it
    is sent.
  - Administrators read a transcript of the prompts, tool calls and outcomes of their organization,
    kept for the retention period of the audit.
---

Organizations must control their use of AI: turn it off, cap its cost, limit its rate and read what
it did. The assistant of the kernel and every extension that calls a model share the same budget,
metering and transcript, instead of one per extension.
