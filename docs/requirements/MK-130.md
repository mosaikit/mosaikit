---
id: MK-130
title: Delete an organization, with its data, its extensions' data and an event to extensions
area: kernel
priority: P2
status: proposed
depends: [MK-085]
acceptance:
  - A platform administrator deletes an organization after a confirmation that names it; its people
    lose access at once and the organization no longer appears anywhere.
  - The kernel raises the event of an organization deleted, and every extension backend subscribed to
    it is called so that it releases what it keeps for the organization.
  - The data of the organization and of its extensions are kept for a retention set by the
    installation, then removed; the audit log records the deletion and the removal.
---

The kernel creates organizations but cannot delete them. Deleting one is delicate: people must lose
access at once, extensions must release what they keep (a map server workspace, files, scheduled
work), and the data must stay for a while before they disappear, so that a mistake can be undone and
audits can be answered.
