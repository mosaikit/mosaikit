---
id: MK-130
title: Delete an organization, with its data, its extensions' data and an event to extensions
area: kernel
priority: P2
status: done
depends: [MK-085]
acceptance:
  - A platform administrator deletes an organization after a confirmation that names it; its people
    lose access at once and the organization no longer appears anywhere.
  - The kernel raises the event of an organization deleted, and every extension backend subscribed to
    it is called so that it releases what it keeps for the organization.
  - The data of the organization and of its extensions are kept for a retention set by the
    installation, then removed; the audit log records the deletion and the removal.
---

The kernel creates organizations but cannot delete them. Deleting one is delicate: people must lose
access at once, extensions must release what they keep (a map server workspace, files, scheduled
work), and the data must stay for a while before they disappear, so that a mistake can be undone and
audits can be answered.

## History

- 2026-10-14: written when MK-085 was closed: its event of an organization deleted moved here,
  because nothing can raise it until organizations can be deleted.
- 2026-10-15: planned in the phase Composition, because the file manager of the common extensions removes the files of a deleted organization (EXT-004); in progress.
- 2026-10-15: done — `DELETE /api/v1/organizations/{slug}?confirmation=<slug>` and the console page
  **Organizations** delete an organization once its slug is written again: it is marked (migration
  V39, `deleted_at`, `removable_from`) and left out of every query of the kernel and of the published
  views, its email domains are released, the sessions of its people that act on it end and their
  access keys in it are revoked (as in MK-125), and `organization.deleted` reaches every subscribed
  extension. Its data stay until `mosaikit.organizations.removal-retention` (30 days, one day at
  least, also checked by the database); then a job of the kernel removes, in one transaction, the
  rows of the organization in the tables of every extension schema, its events and the organization
  with its kernel data, and queues the content of its files for the clean-up of the store. The audit
  log records `organization.deleted` and `organization.removed`. Extensions release what they keep
  outside on the event, and destroy nothing. Tests `OrganizationDeletionTest` and
  `organizations.test.ts`.
