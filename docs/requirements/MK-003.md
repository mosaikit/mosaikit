---
id: MK-003
title: Local accounts, self-registration and first administrator
area: security
priority: P1
status: done
depends: [MK-010]
acceptance:
  - Passwords are stored as PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 hashes with at least 600,000 iterations and a random
    salt.
  - Registration requires a password of 12 to 128 characters and is refused where the organization
    does not allow it.
  - Email addresses are case-insensitive and unique.
  - Wrong passwords and unknown usernames give the same answer.
  - The first platform administrator is created at first start from configuration.
---

People can sign in without an external identity provider, which portable installations and emergency
access require. Organizations decide whether people can register by themselves.
