area: kernel

acceptance:
  - All shows the first results of every provider; a pill shows only that provider, with paging.
  - A result is never shown to a person who cannot read it.
  - A provider that does not answer in time is shown as incomplete and the others are shown anyway.
---

The search box finds people, teams, messages, files and the data of the apps; one pill per provider
narrows the search. Providers are declared with the point search.provider and search their tables
with PostgreSQL full-text search under row-level security. See ADR-0030.

## History

- 2026-10-15: in progress.
- 2026-10-15: done. The shell opens the results of the whole organization on `/search`, with All and
  one pill per provider; the kernel provides people and teams with PostgreSQL full-text search, and
  extensions add providers with the typed point `search.provider`, called with the credentials of the
  person; a result that names a resource the person may not read is dropped (MK-135, MK-136), and a
  provider slower than `mosaikit.search.timeout` is shown as incomplete. Tests: `GlobalSearchTest`,
  `SearchProvidersTest`, `SearchTest`, `search.test.ts`.
