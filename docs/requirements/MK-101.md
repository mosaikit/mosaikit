---
id: MK-101
title: "A home page of widgets contributed by extensions, per role and for visitors"
area: shell
priority: P2
status: done
decisions: [ADR-0039]
acceptance:
  - Extensions contribute home widgets declaring the roles, and whether anonymous visitors, they
    appear for.
  - A platform administrator, an organization administrator and a member see different sets of
    widgets on the same installation.
  - An anonymous visitor sees only the widgets declared public.
---

After sign-in each role should find what needs its attention, and visitors without an account should
find the public face of the installation. The frame of the home page is generic, and its content
comes from extensions.

## History

- 2026-10-13: written from the needs of a product built on Mosaikit, reviewed from its backlog; 2 of
  its requirements depend on it.
- 2026-10-15: in progress.
- 2026-10-15: implemented. The kernel owns the extension point `home.widget`, typed by a JSON Schema
  as the points of MK-134 (`title`, `element`, `roles`, `public`, `size`, `order`, `permission`); the
  marketplace refuses a widget that does not match, with the reason, and the shell never receives
  one. `GET /api/v1/shell/extensions` gives each person only the widgets of their roles, and
  `GET /api/v1/shell/home/public` gives visitors only the public ones, which the shell shows under
  the sign-in page; on the home page a widget of an extension no longer active in the organization
  disappears without a reload (MK-133). Tests: `HomeWidgetsTest` (a platform administrator, an
  organization administrator, a member and a visitor of the same installation; a widget refused
  with the reason, one copied into the directory never served), `home.test.ts`. Public widgets get
  no session: data for visitors waits for public pages (MK-089).
