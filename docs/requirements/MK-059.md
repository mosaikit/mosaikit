---
id: MK-059
title: Tamper-evident audit log written by the kernel and the extensions
area: security
priority: P1
status: in-progress
acceptance:
  - An extension writes an entry with actor, action, object, time and channel through kernel-api,
    and it appears in the audit log of the organization.
  - Organization administrators list and filter the entries of their own organization only.
  - The API offers no way to change or delete an entry, and a verification endpoint reports an
    altered entry at its exact position.
  - Entries older than the retention of the organization are deleted automatically, never earlier
    than a minimum set by the installation.
---

Sensitive actions of people, automated jobs and the assistant must leave a durable record of who did
what on which object, when and through which channel. One audit log per organization, written by the
kernel and by the extensions, spares each extension keeping its own and gives administrators one
place to read it. Auditors also ask for a declared retention and for proof that no entry was
altered.
