title: "Plans with features, limits and entitlements, and their public price list"
area: marketplace
priority: P1
status: proposed
acceptance:
  - A plan defined by the platform administrator, with its price and quotas, appears in the public
    price list and at registration, and a change of price shows everywhere at once.
  - A feature outside the plan of the organization is refused with a problem detail that names the
    feature and the plan that unlocks it.
  - An action beyond a quota of the plan is refused with a problem detail that names the quota.
  - Concurrent invitations on the last seat admit exactly one person.
---

A product sold by subscription defines plans with features, limits and prices, and assigns each
organization to one. One entitlement resolver answers every check and explains its refusals, and the
public price list comes from the same source, so that no price is written by hand twice. Enforcement
can be turned off where nothing is sold, and a change of plan never acts retroactively.
