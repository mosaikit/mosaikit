title: Trash with restore for deleted resources
area: kernel
priority: P1
status: done
depends: [MK-054]
acceptance:
  - A deleted resource appears in the trash of its owner and of the organization administrators.
  - Restoring it brings it back with its shares.
  - Resources in the trash longer than the configured period are deleted for good.
---

Deletions by mistake are common. Resources of extensions go to a trash, from which their owner or an
administrator restores them for a configured period.

## History

- 2026-10-13: written from the needs of a product built on Mosaikit, reviewed from its backlog; 1 of
  its requirements depend on it.
- 2026-10-15: in progress.
- 2026-10-15: done — an extension moves a resource to the trash with the bean `Trash` instead of
  deleting it, and the resources inside it (by the parent of the registry) go with it (migration
  V41). While in the trash it gives no level and its links answer not found; its owner, and the
  administrators of the organization, see it on the page **Trash** of the shell (`GET
  /api/v1/trash`) and restore it with its grants and links, or delete it for good. A job deletes it
  for good after `mosaikit.trash.retention` (30 days, one day at least, also checked by the
  database). The events `resource.trashed`, `resource.restored` and `resource.purged` tell the
  extensions; the audit log records them. Tests `TrashTest` and `trash.test.ts`.
