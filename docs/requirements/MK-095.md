id: MK-095
title: Trash with restore for deleted resources
area: kernel
priority: P1
status: in-progress
depends: [MK-054]
acceptance:
  - A deleted resource appears in the trash of its owner and of the organization administrators.
  - Restoring it brings it back with its shares.
  - Resources in the trash longer than the configured period are deleted for good.
---

Deletions by mistake are common. Resources of extensions go to a trash, from which their owner or an
administrator restores them for a configured period.
