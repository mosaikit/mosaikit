---
id: MK-066
title: Sign-in with national digital identities through the organization realm
area: security
priority: P1
status: proposed
acceptance:
  - An organization configures a national identity such as SPID in its realm, and a person signs in
    through a test provider at the required assurance level.
  - The same flow works with a second national identity, such as CIE.
  - The sign-in page shows the prescribed button and the choice of provider.
---

Services that public bodies offer to citizens must accept the national digital identities of their
country, such as SPID and CIE in Italy, with their SAML 2 profiles, metadata, assurance levels and
prescribed sign-in button. The organization configures them in its realm, so that the kernel and the
extensions see an ordinary signed-in person. It extends MK-012 with identity providers that follow a
national profile.
