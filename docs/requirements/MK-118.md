---
id: MK-118
title: Data residency and a no-egress switch per organization
area: security
priority: P2
status: proposed
acceptance:
  - A page of the organization declares where its database, files, backups and logs are kept.
  - With the no-egress switch on, the assistant refuses hosted language models outside the boundary
    and says why.
  - Extensions read the switch through the SDK and declare which of their features send content
    outside.
---

For critical data an organization must know where its data are kept and be able to forbid that any
content leaves the installation. The assistant and the extensions honor the switch.
