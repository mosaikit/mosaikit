---
id: MK-070
title: Self-service profile of a person and sign-out from all devices
area: shell
priority: P2
status: proposed
acceptance:
  - A person changes their name, phone, biography and avatar, and the shell and other members show
    the change after saving.
  - Signing out from all devices ends the sessions of the person on other browsers at their next
    request.
---

People keep their own profile up to date (name, email, phone, biography, avatar) without asking an
administrator, and end all their sessions when a device is lost. It extends the personal settings of
MK-027 with the profile and the sessions of the person.
