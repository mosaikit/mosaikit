---
id: MK-043
title: Connectors to external file services (ext-files-*)
area: extensions
priority: P2
status: planned
depends: [MK-042]
acceptance:
  - An organization adds a connection to a WebDAV service and its people browse and open its files
    in app-files.
  - A file of a connection is read with the credentials configured for it and never copied into the
    storage of the kernel unless a person saves a copy.
---

Connectors are extensions of app-files that list, read and, when allowed, write the files of an
external service without copying them: GeoNode, WebDAV and Nextcloud, CKAN, another S3. See
ADR-0029.
