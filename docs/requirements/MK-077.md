title: "Subscriptions, invoices, payment methods, electronic invoicing and credits"
area: marketplace
priority: P2
status: proposed
depends: [MK-075]
acceptance:
  - Invoices, payment method and invoicing data of an organization are on one page, and a change of
    plan made in the customer portal changes the plan of the organization.
  - An invoice with the tax data of the customer is sent to the national electronic invoicing
    exchange, and its outcome is shown.
  - An assistant call without AI credit left is refused with a message, and a completed call lowers
    the balance shown.
---

Selling needs subscriptions that customers change from a portal, invoices and payment methods on one
page, and the tax data and electronic invoicing that some countries require, such as the exchange
system (SDI) of Italy. Metered features need a balance, and the assistant needs AI credits with
top-up, monthly grant and a charge after each call, stopping clearly when none is left.
