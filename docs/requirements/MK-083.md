---
id: MK-083
title: Extension backends as registered external services
area: extensions
priority: P2
status: proposed
depends: [MK-080]
acceptance:
  - An extension declares an external backend, and the kernel proxies its calls with credentials
    kept encrypted.
  - When the health check of the backend fails, its app shows as off with the reason instead of a
    blank page.
  - Calls of the backend to the platform above its rate limit are refused with 429.
---

Backends of third parties, written in any language, run outside the process of the kernel. They are
registered with their base address, credentials, health check, rate limits and the behavior to show
when they are down, and the kernel proxies the calls to them.
