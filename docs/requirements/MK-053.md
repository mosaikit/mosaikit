id: MK-053
title: The texts of contributions in the language of the person
area: extensions
priority: P1
status: done
depends: [MK-027, MK-052]
acceptance:
  - A contribution declares i18n with texts by language tag; a block that is not texts by language
    makes the manifest invalid.
  - The app bar, the home page and the settings show the names of apps and sections in the language
    of the person, and change with it.
  - Without a text for the language, the attribute itself is shown.
---

The names that extensions give to their apps and to their sections of the settings show in the
language of the person, not only in the one of the manifest. A contribution gives its text
attributes in other languages in an i18n block by language tag; the shell uses the language of the
person, then its main language, then the attribute itself, and follows a change of language at once.
