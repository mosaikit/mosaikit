---
id: MK-110
title: "Assistant retrieval over indexed content, with each figure bound to its source"
area: ai
priority: P1
status: proposed
acceptance:
  - An extension contributes documents that describe its objects to the assistant index of the
    organization, and the assistant retrieves them before answering.
  - Documents of objects the person cannot see are never retrieved for that person.
  - Each figure in an answer carries a reference to the tool call and result that produced it, which
    the person can open.
  - A figure that cannot be bound to a tool result is not shown, and the answer says so.
---

The assistant should know the content of the organization, not only the open page. Extensions feed
an index with descriptions of their objects, and the assistant retrieves from it, filtered by the
rights of the person. In regulated settings an answer is admissible only if every figure in it can
be traced to the data and the call that produced it. It extends the assistant of MK-024 with
retrieval and sources.
