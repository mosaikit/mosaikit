---
id: MK-100
title: Embed an extension view in a page of another site
area: shell
priority: P1
status: planned
depends: [MK-055]
acceptance:
  - A view of an extension opened in embed mode shows no app bar and no top bar.
  - An iframe on a site listed by the organization loads the view; on any other site the browser
    refuses it (frame-ancestors).
  - An embedded view of a resource shared publicly opens without sign-in, and one that is not shared
    asks for sign-in or is refused.
---

Organizations publish views of their apps inside their own websites. The shell serves the view of an
extension without its frame, in an iframe, only on the sites the organization allows, and with the
access given by the sharing of the resource.
