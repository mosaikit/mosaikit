---
id: MK-100
title: Embed an extension view in a page of another site
area: shell
priority: P1
status: done
depends: [MK-055]
acceptance:
  - A view of an extension opened in embed mode shows no app bar and no top bar.
  - An iframe on a site listed by the organization loads the view; on any other site the browser
    refuses it (frame-ancestors).
  - An embedded view of a resource shared publicly opens without sign-in, and one that is not shared
    asks for sign-in or is refused.
---

Organizations publish views of their apps inside their own websites. The shell serves the view of an
extension without its frame, in an iframe, only on the sites the organization allows, and with the
access given by the sharing of the resource.

## History

- 2026-10-13: written from the needs of a product built on Mosaikit, reviewed from its backlog; 1 of
  its requirements depend on it.
- 2026-10-15: in progress.
- 2026-10-15: done. The embed mode of the shell, `/embed/<slug>/...`, shows a resource shared with
  the public (`public/<extension>/<type>/<id>`), a resource opened with a link of the organization
  (`shared/<secret>`) or the view of an app with its state (MK-099, `app/...`), without the app bar
  and the top bar (acceptance 1). The administrators of the organization list the sites that may
  frame it (**Embedded views**, `/api/v1/organizations/{slug}/embed-sites`, table `embed_site` of
  migration V49, owner-only and audited); the kernel sends `frame-ancestors 'self' <sites>` for
  those addresses only and keeps `X-Frame-Options: DENY` and `frame-ancestors 'none'` everywhere else
  (acceptance 2). The list is per organization and separate from the external origins of MK-128,
  which say what the browser may contact, not who may frame the installation. A request without an
  account that names the organization (`X-Mosaikit-Public`) gets the level of the public, also
  through a folder above (MK-136), never for a resource in the trash (MK-095); a resource not shared
  answers not found and the frame offers to sign in in a tab of its own; the session cookies stay
  `SameSite=Strict`, so a frame of another site never carries a session (acceptance 3). Verified by
  `EmbedTest`, `EmbedFramingTest`, `ContentSecurityPolicyTest`, `embed.test.ts` and the end-to-end
  test `30-embed`, which frames the views from a listed site and from another one.
