id: MK-116
title: Scheduled backups with restore tested automatically
area: distribution
priority: P2
status: proposed
acceptance:
  - A scheduled job restores the latest backup on a clean environment and records its outcome and
    duration.
  - A failed restore test notifies the platform administrator.
  - Extensions declare additional stores, and those are included in backup and restore.
---

A backup is proven only by a restore. Installations need a periodic automated restore on a clean
environment, declared and measured recovery point and time objectives, and a copy kept off site;
extensions add their own stores to it.
