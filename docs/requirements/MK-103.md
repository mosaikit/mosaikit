---
id: MK-103
title: "Guided tours of pages, started on demand"
area: shell
priority: P3
status: proposed
acceptance:
  - Extensions declare tours for their pages, with localized steps anchored to elements.
  - The help command of the top bar starts the tour of the current page, and no tour starts by
    itself.
  - A step whose element is missing is skipped instead of breaking the tour.
---

Each page of each app can offer a localized tour from one help command of the shell. Tours are
offered, never imposed: they start only when the person asks.

## History

- 2026-10-13: written from the needs of a product built on Mosaikit, reviewed from its backlog; 1 of
  its requirements depend on it.
