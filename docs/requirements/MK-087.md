---
id: MK-087
title: Outgoing mail with attachments for extensions
area: kernel
priority: P2
status: planned
acceptance:
  - An extension sends a mail with a PDF attachment through kernel-api, and the recipient receives
    it with the attachment.
  - The sender shown is the one configured for the organization.
  - Every mail sent by an extension is recorded in the audit with the extension, the recipients and
    the time.
---

Extensions send mail to people and to other addresses (reports, digests, invitations), with
attachments. They use the mail settings of the installation and the sender of the organization, and
what they send is audited.
