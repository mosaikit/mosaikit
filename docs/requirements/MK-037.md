title: The assistant as a bot in chats and channels
area: ai
priority: P2
status: planned
depends: [MK-024, MK-034, MK-036]
acceptance:
  - A person asks the assistant in a chat and gets an answer from the tools of the active
    extensions.
  - When mentioned in a channel the assistant answers in the thread, and a change it proposes waits
    for the confirmation of the person who asked.
---

The assistant of the kernel appears as a contact in app-chat and answers when mentioned in a channel
of app-teams, with the rights of the person who asks; its changes stay drafts until confirmed. See
ADR-0022 and ADR-0027.
