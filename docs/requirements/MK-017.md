---
id: MK-017
title: Membership of several organizations
area: security
priority: P2
status: done
depends: [MK-002, MK-003, MK-012]
acceptance:
  - A person with a local account in one organization can be added to a second organization and sign
    in to it through its realm with the same email address.
  - Every request acts on one organization of which the person is a member; data of other
    organizations are never visible.
  - An organization that requires its realm refuses local passwords for its data, while the same
    person keeps signing in with a password to organizations that allow it.
---

A person has one account and may belong to several organizations, with roles in each. The
organization of a request is chosen by its sub-domain or by an organization selector in the UI, and
each organization decides how its members sign in (local password, its realm, or both). See
ADR-0016.
