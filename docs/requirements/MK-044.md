---
id: MK-044
title: Configurable retention of messages and files, with audit
area: security
priority: P1
status: planned
depends: [MK-034, MK-036, MK-041]
acceptance:
  - Messages and files older than the retention of the organization are deleted by a daily job, and
    the audit records what was deleted and when.
  - A change of the retention is recorded in the audit with the person who made it.
---

Each organization sets how long messages and files are kept; the kernel deletes what expires and
records every deletion in the audit. eDiscovery is a later step. See ADR-0027 and ADR-0029.
