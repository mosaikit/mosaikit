title: "Cookie consent with categories, revocation and proof"
area: shell
priority: P3
status: proposed
acceptance:
  - No non-technical script loads before the person chooses.
  - The banner offers separate categories, and refusing takes one action, like accepting.
  - A link that is always reachable reopens the choice, and the consent given is stored with its
    date.
---

Public pages with measurement or third-party content need consent per category. Nothing
non-technical runs before the choice, refusing is as easy as accepting, the choice can be revoked at
any time, and what was consented can be proven.
