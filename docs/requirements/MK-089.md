---
id: MK-089
title: "Pages and endpoints of extensions open to anonymous visitors, per organization"
area: security
priority: P3
status: proposed
depends: [MK-090]
acceptance:
  - An extension declares in its manifest the pages and endpoints that answer anonymous requests;
    all the others still require sign-in.
  - An anonymous visitor reaches a declared public page of an organization through its sub-domain
    without seeing the sign-in page.
  - With the public surface of the organization turned off, every anonymous page and endpoint of
    that organization is refused.
---

Products need a public surface, such as a public catalog, a public home or shared links, that
visitors use without an account. Extensions declare a narrow set of anonymous pages and endpoints,
and each organization has one switch that turns its whole public surface off.
