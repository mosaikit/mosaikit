---
id: MK-072
title: Administration pages of the platform and of organizations on one model
area: shell
priority: P1
status: in-progress
decisions: [ADR-0039]
acceptance:
  - A console reachable only by platform administrators shows organizations, people, active
    extensions, system metrics and security events such as failed sign-ins.
  - A guided flow creates an organization with its administrator and sign-in method; an organization
    can be suspended, reactivated and deleted, and members of a suspended one cannot sign in to it.
  - Extensions contribute panels to the console through an extension point.
  - Every administration page shows metrics on top, filters above the table, actions on each row and
    the detail of a row in a side pane.
---

Platform administrators need one place for the state of the installation: organizations and their
usage, people, metrics, security events, the audit log and settings. Organization administrators
need the same pages for their own organization, including the accounts of its members. All these
pages share one shape, so that the panels added by extensions fit in. It extends MK-002, where the
platform administrator lists and creates organizations, with their lifecycle and the rest of the
administration.
