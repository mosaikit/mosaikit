---
id: MK-065
title: "Second factor, lockout, password policy and sessions for local accounts"
area: security
priority: P1
status: proposed
acceptance:
  - A local account with a second factor cannot sign in with the password alone.
  - Repeated failed sign-ins get a growing wait and a 429 with Retry-After.
  - A person sees their active sessions, and revoking one ends it at the next request; changing the
    password or suspending the account revokes all of them.
  - An organization raises the minimum length of passwords and requires the second factor for a
    role, and its members are held to both.
---

Local accounts, the emergency administrator included, need a time-based second factor, a growing
wait after failed attempts, limits on sign-in and recovery, and a list of sessions the person can
revoke. Each organization can set a stricter policy for its members. It extends the local accounts
of MK-003, which define how passwords are stored and how long they are, with these protections.
