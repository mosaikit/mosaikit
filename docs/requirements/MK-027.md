---
id: MK-027
title: 'Personal settings: theme, language and active organization'
area: shell
priority: P1
status: done
depends: [MK-017, MK-025]
acceptance:
  - Theme and language chosen by a person apply at once to the shell and to every extension, and are
    kept at the next sign-in.
  - A person with several organizations switches organization from the menu without signing in
    again; the apps reload for that organization.
  - A section contributed by an extension appears in the settings only for the people allowed by the
    extension.
---

A settings page of the kernel lets a person choose the theme (light, dark, high contrast, themes of
extensions), the language and the active organization; extensions add sections with the point
settings.section. The menu of the person also switches organization; the presence comes with MK-033.
The shell is in English and Italian; the language goes to extensions as context.locale.
