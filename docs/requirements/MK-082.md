---
id: MK-082
title: Different versions of an extension for different organizations
area: extensions
priority: P2
status: proposed
acceptance:
  - Two versions of the same extension are installed, and each organization uses the one assigned to
    it.
  - An organization moves to the newer version without affecting the others.
  - A version incompatible with the kernel shows as unavailable, with the reason.
---

One organization must be able to stay on a version of an extension while another moves to the next
one. An upgrade is then tried and rolled out one organization at a time, and an organization that
depends on the old behavior is not forced to move with the others.

## History

- 2026-10-13: written from the needs of a product built on Mosaikit, reviewed from its backlog; 2 of
  its requirements depend on it.
