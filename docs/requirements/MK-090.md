---
id: MK-090
title: Rate limits per client on anonymous and public endpoints
area: security
priority: P3
status: proposed
acceptance:
  - Requests from one address above the configured rate on anonymous endpoints get 429 with a
    Retry-After header.
  - Signed-in requests are not counted against the anonymous limit.
  - An extension declares a stricter limit for an expensive anonymous endpoint, and it applies.
---

Anonymous endpoints can be called by anyone, as often as they like. Without a limit per address, a
crawler or an aggregator can starve the people who have an account.
