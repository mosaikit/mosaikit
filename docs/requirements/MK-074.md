---
id: MK-074
title: "Branding per organization, with a live editor of the theme tokens"
area: shell
priority: P1
status: planned
acceptance:
  - An organization administrator, and no other member, uploads a logo (light and dark), an icon and
    a favicon of up to 2 MB and sets a display name and an accent color.
  - Members of that organization see its logo, name and colors after sign-in and on its sign-in
    page; members of another organization do not.
  - A change of a theme token in the editor shows on the open application before it is saved.
  - An appearance chosen by the person wins over the one of the organization, which wins over the
    one of the installation.
---

In an installation with several organizations, each wants its own mark on the sign-in page and in
the shell, beyond the brand of the installation. Administrators also adjust the colors of a theme
without writing an extension, previewing the change on the real application. It extends MK-052,
where the theme of the installation names the product and brings its logo, to each organization.
