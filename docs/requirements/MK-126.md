---
id: MK-126
title: Extensions act for an organization without a person, in jobs and at start
area: kernel
priority: P2
status: proposed
acceptance:
  - An extension runs work for one organization outside any request, such as at start, on a schedule
    or after an event, and the beans of the kernel (resources, files, sharing, quotas, audit) act in
    that organization under its row-level security.
  - What such work registers or stores is owned by the organization, and the audit log records the
    extension as the actor with the channel JOB.
  - An extension cannot use this to act in an organization where it is not installed or whose apps
    are turned off.
---

Extensions need to do work that no person asked for: load sample data when an organization is
created, refresh a catalog every night, finish a long import after the request that started it has
ended. Today the beans of the kernel take the organization from the request, so such work either
waits for a person to open a page or runs without any organization and sees no data. The kernel
offers a way to run a piece of work for a given organization, with the same rules as a request but
no person, owned by the organization and audited as a job.

## History

- 2026-10-13: written after a product built on Mosaikit had to load the sample data of an
  organization on the first visit of an administrator instead of when the organization was created.
- 2026-10-13: a first part done with MK-085: the handlers of events of the platform run for the
  organization of their event without a person, under its row-level security, own what they register
  and are audited as the extension through the channel JOB, never for an organization that turned the
  extension off. Work at start and on a schedule still has no API.
