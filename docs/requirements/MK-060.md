---
id: MK-060
title: Personal access keys for scripts and external clients
area: security
priority: P1
status: in-progress
depends: [MK-056]
acceptance:
  - A person creates a named key and sees its secret once; the list shows its prefix, creation, last
    use and expiry.
  - A call with a revoked key is refused at the next request, while the sessions of the person on
    other devices go on.
  - A key created without the write switch is refused on every action with side effects.
  - A service validates a key through an endpoint of the kernel and receives the person and the
    organization it belongs to.
---

Scripts, desktop and command-line tools and MCP clients cannot use the session of the browser.
People need long-lived personal keys that act with their rights, can be revoked one by one, and are
checked by the kernel for any service that receives them.
