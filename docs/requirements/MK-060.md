title: Personal access keys for scripts and external clients
area: security
priority: P1
status: done
depends: [MK-056]
acceptance:
  - A person creates a named key and sees its secret once; the list shows its prefix, creation, last
    use and expiry.
  - A call with a revoked key is refused at the next request, while the sessions of the person on
    other devices go on.
  - A key created without the write switch is refused on every action with side effects.
  - A service validates a key through an endpoint of the kernel and receives the person and the
    organization it belongs to.
---

Scripts, desktop and command-line tools and MCP clients cannot use the session of the browser.
People need long-lived personal keys that act with their rights, can be revoked one by one, and are
checked by the kernel for any service that receives them.

## History

- 2026-10-13: written from the needs of a product built on Mosaikit, reviewed from its backlog; 3 of
  its requirements depend on it.
- 2026-10-13: in progress — personal access keys checked by the kernel, with a validation endpoint for services.
- 2026-10-13: done — keys in `access_key` (migration V22), kept as a digest, sent as `Authorization:
  Bearer mk_...`; they act for the person in the organization chosen at creation, with the roles of
  the person there at each call; read-only without the write switch (kernel and extension APIs);
  revoked at once, by the person, by a suspension and by the removal from the organization; no key
  reaches sessions or keys. The audit log (MK-059) records through the `API_KEY` channel every
  refused request and the first use of a key in each hour, the creations and every revocation, and
  every other entry written during a request made with a key has that channel. Services validate a
  key with `GET /api/v1/access-keys/current`, called with the key itself, until service identities
  (MK-124) give them credentials of their own. Tests `AccessKeyTest`,
  `AccessKeyRegistryTest`, `access-keys.test.ts` and e2e `23-access-keys`.
