acceptance:
  - A remembered person is still signed in after closing the browser; after signing out, a copy of
    the cookie signs nobody in.
  - Without "remember me", closing the browser signs the person out.
  - The sign-in page has no WCAG 2.1 AA violation found by axe-core, in both themes, and fits a
    phone.
  - With mosaikit.ui.theme=pa the page uses the colors and the font of Bootstrap Italia, with text
    contrast of at least 4.5:1.
---

The shell signs people in on a page of its own, built with the Fluent UI web components of
@mosaikit/ui and the theme of the installation (mosaikit.ui.theme): the default one, or the one for
public administrations in the style of Bootstrap Italia. A person who signs in with a password can
ask to be remembered on the browser: an HttpOnly cookie, kept in the database as a digest, signs
them in again when the session ended, for mosaikit.accounts.remember-for, until they sign out.
