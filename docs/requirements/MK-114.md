---
id: MK-114
title: External penetration test of the platform
area: security
priority: P1
status: proposed
acceptance:
  - An external tester has tried to cross the boundary between organizations through the API, the
    real-time channel and the endpoints of extensions, and the report lists the results.
  - The tester has tried to load or alter an unsigned or tampered extension package, and the kernel
    refused it.
  - Every high or critical finding is fixed and its test repeated with a passing result.
---

Products built on the platform inherit its boundary between organizations, its sign-in, its sharing
links and its extension loader. An external penetration test of those parts, with fixes and a
repeated test, is needed before deliveries to public bodies and enterprises.

- 2026-10-13: written from the needs of a product built on Mosaikit, reviewed from its backlog; 1 of
