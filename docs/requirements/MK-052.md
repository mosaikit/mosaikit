---
id: MK-052
title: The theme of the installation names the product and brings its logo
area: extensions
priority: P1
status: done
depends: [MK-028]
acceptance:
  - A theme extension declares theme.product and theme.logo (light, and optionally dark); a name or
    a file the shell cannot show makes the manifest invalid.
  - With that theme as the theme of the installation, /api/v1/system/info gives its product name and
    the addresses of its logos, which are served before sign-in.
  - The bar of the shell, the sign-in page, the title and the icon of the page show them; on a dark
    background, and in the bar of the shell, the logo for dark backgrounds.
  - Without a theme that names a product, the shell keeps the name and the mark of Mosaikit.
---

A product built on Mosaikit presents itself with its own name and logo, not those of Mosaikit. A
theme extension can name the product and bring its logo, an SVG or PNG file for light backgrounds
and optionally one for dark backgrounds. When it is the theme of the installation
(mosaikit.ui.theme), the shell shows that name and logo in its bar and on the sign-in page, and uses
them as the title and the icon of the page. They belong to the installation: a person who chooses
another theme in the settings keeps them.
