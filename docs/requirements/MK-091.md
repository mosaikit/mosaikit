---
id: MK-091
title: Editorial public pages of an organization
area: shell
priority: P3
status: proposed
depends: [MK-089]
acceptance:
  - An organization administrator writes a page in Markdown, and the public route serves it as HTML
    sanitized on the server; a script tag in the source never reaches the browser.
  - The public home shows the declared blocks, those of extensions included, in the declared order,
    with the configured menu and footer.
  - A page missing in the language of the visitor falls back to the main language of the
    organization, and the editor lists the missing translations.
  - A page can be saved as a draft, previewed as an anonymous visitor sees it, and restored to an
    earlier version.
---

Organizations need their own public texts (about, help, contacts, legal notices), a public home and
a menu, without editing the files of the product. Text written by people is shown to anonymous
visitors and next to the sign-in form, so it is sanitized on the server. Images and attachments of a
page count on the storage quota of the organization.
