title: "Prices, licences, trials, cart and orders in the marketplace"
area: marketplace
priority: P1
status: proposed
depends: [MK-077]
acceptance:
  - Each paid entry shows how it is paid before checkout.
  - A trial shows the remaining days and turns the extension off by itself when it expires.
  - An order keeps the price and the description of the item at the time of purchase, and its
    invoice can be downloaded as PDF.
---

The marketplace lists extensions but does not sell them. Entries need a price model (included,
one-off, metered, on request), trials that end by themselves, a cart and orders that keep a snapshot
of what was bought. It extends the signed catalogs of MK-022 with the commercial side of an entry.
