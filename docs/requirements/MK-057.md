---
id: MK-057
title: Explain who can access a resource and why
area: security
priority: P2
status: proposed
depends: [MK-055, MK-056]
acceptance:
  - The access view of a resource lists every person with access and the grant that gives it (group,
    direct, default of the organization, link).
  - Revoking the listed grant removes the person from the list when no other grant applies.
---

Audits and owners ask, for each resource, who can see it and through what: a group, a direct grant,
the default of the organization or a link. The permission resolver returns the reason with each
answer, so that the access view of a resource can show it next to every person.
