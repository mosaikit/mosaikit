---
id: MK-071
title: Offboarding a member with the transfer of their resources
area: kernel
priority: P1
status: in-progress
depends: [MK-054]
acceptance:
  - Removing a member first shows the resources they own, per extension.
  - The administrator chooses a new owner, and after the removal those resources belong to that
    person with their shares intact.
  - Extensions receive the transfer through an extension point and update their ownership.
---

When a person leaves an organization, what they own must neither become orphaned nor disappear with
them. Before the removal the administrator sees the resources of the member across extensions and
chooses who takes them over.
