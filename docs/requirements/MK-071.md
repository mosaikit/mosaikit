---
id: MK-071
title: Offboarding a member with the transfer of their resources
area: kernel
priority: P1
status: done
depends: [MK-054]
acceptance:
  - Removing a member first shows the resources they own, per extension.
  - The administrator chooses a new owner, and after the removal those resources belong to that
    person with their shares intact.
  - Extensions receive the transfer through an extension point and update their ownership.
---

When a person leaves an organization, what they own must neither become orphaned nor disappear with
them. Before the removal the administrator sees the resources of the member across extensions and
chooses who takes them over.

## History

- 2026-10-13: written from the needs of a product built on Mosaikit, reviewed from its backlog; 1 of
  its requirements depend on it.
- 2026-10-15: in progress.
- 2026-10-15: done. `GET /api/v1/organizations/{slug}/members/{email}/resources` lists what a member
  owns per extension; `DELETE .../members/{email}?transferTo=` gives it to another member in the
  registry, grants intact, and calls the extension point `OwnershipTransfers` of `kernel-api` for each
  active extension concerned, in the transaction of the removal. The administrators of the
  organization remove members too, from **Permissions**, **People**.
