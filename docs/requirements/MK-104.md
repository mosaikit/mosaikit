---
id: MK-104
title: Responsive shell with a bottom bar and bottom sheets on phones
area: shell
priority: P1
status: proposed
acceptance:
  - At 390 px of width no page of the shell scrolls horizontally, and the app bar moves to a bottom
    bar.
  - The UI kit offers a bottom sheet that closes with Escape, a downward swipe or a drag below a
    third of its height.
  - Three breakpoints are documented tokens that extensions can rely on.
---

Products on the platform must work from large monitors to phones. The frame of the shell and a
bottom sheet component exist once, so that extension pages only adapt their own content to the
declared breakpoints. It extends MK-025, where the app bar becomes a bottom navigation at phone
width, with the bottom sheet and breakpoints that extensions can use.
